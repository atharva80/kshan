After everything is uploaded

  1. Sort: in Immich, long-press photos to select them → + → Add to album. Make as many albums as you like.
  2. Find: Immich's search finds people, places, things, and words said or written in videos.
  3. Copy back to a pendrive: open an album → ⋮ → Download. Big albums download in parts. Then move the files to the pendrive with your phone's Files app.
  4. Don't delete originals until the server has a backup.
